7609 Creekbluff Dr · Austin, TX 78750

Nine Years, One House

A single-family home on the Bull Creek greenbelt has stood gutted and unoccupied for close to a decade, through nine City of Austin code cases that each closed without resolving it. This is the running record: what the City did, what the neighborhood did, and every document behind it.

Maintained by Mark Garcia, President, Bull Creek Home Owners Association
Grounded in City code records, public-information responses, Texas filings and dated photographs
The short version
  • A 3,742 sq ft house on the Bull Creek greenbelt has sat gutted to the studs and unoccupied since roughly 2016. Neighbors have been complaining to the City since 2017.
  • Austin has opened ten code cases on it. None has ever reached a hearing. Each one closed — several on "voluntary compliance" the neighborhood disputes, one because the file "is over a year old" and the City could not find the folder.
  • Ownership moves whenever enforcement closes in. A 2018 citation was followed by the deed leaving the cited company; in July 2025 the property's two prior owners of record formed an LLC together and deeded the house into it 28 days later.
  • In August 2026 the City closed its case as compliant on a one-sentence inspector's note — "The repairs made to the exterior of the structure" — that names no repair. The City's own photographs from that day show the cited porch posts unchanged, and it never photographed the wall it cleared.
  • The neighborhood published the City's own 266-page file and photographs taken sixteen days after the closure showing the conditions still in place, then pressed the District 10 council office.
  • On September 24, 2026 the City reopened the property under a new case with nine violations — including, for the first time in nine years, the inside of the house: a rodent infestation and interior drywall damage. Every repair now requires a permit first, which forecloses another paper closure.
Where it stands todayAll compliance deadlines expired in October 2026 and case CV‑2026‑126219 remains open — the first time a case on this property has stayed open past its deadline instead of closing. District 10 has committed in writing that unresolved citations "will escalate to judicial action." Nothing further has been received from the City.

About this record

This site is maintained by the president of the Bull Creek Home Owners Association, the lead correspondent with the City on this property since 2019. It is journalistic in nature: it assembles the verifiable record from public documents, separates confirmed facts from the neighborhood's contentions, and frames open questions as questions.

Timeline entries describing City action are drawn from code-enforcement records obtained under the Texas Public Information Act and published here in full. Entries describing correspondence are drawn from email held by the author; City staff are quoted verbatim and identified by name and title as public employees acting in their official capacity, while private individuals' contact details and the raw correspondence files are deliberately not published.

This report does not allege criminal conduct by any person or entity, and makes no claim about the intent of any City employee or property owner. It is not affiliated with the City of Austin or any governmental body.
