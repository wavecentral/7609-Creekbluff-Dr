Please write your own middle paragraph

Council offices weigh ten personal notes far more heavily than fifty identical ones. One or two specific sentences is all it takes. Things worth mentioning:

  • What you can see from your yard, or how close you live to it
  • How many years you have watched it sit
  • Any complaints you have filed, and what came of them
  • Concerns about fire, rodents, children getting in, or snakes off the greenbelt
  • What it has meant for your property value or your peace of mind
